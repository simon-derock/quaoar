# Coverage test on real SME prospectuses, 10 Oct 2026

**Written before any file in this sample was downloaded or run.** The sample, the measures and the rules below were fixed first. Every result is reported, whatever it shows.

## Question
How many real SME prospectuses can Quaoar read end to end, and how often do its checks flag an ordinary company? The first part needs no SerpApi credit and no model call, so it can be run at scale.

## Sample
- **Frame.** Every final red herring prospectus or prospectus that nine fixed searches of `sharekhan.com` returned: the files coded `PR…`. Draft prospectuses (`PD…`) are left out. The listing is `coverage-2026-10-10-sample.txt`, in the order found: 34 PDFs under `/MediaGalary/IPODocuments/`.
- **Searches.** Run through a web search restricted to `sharekhan.com`:
  1. `IPO "RHP" pdf SME IPO 2026 Emerge prospectus`
  2. `Red Herring Prospectus BSE SME platform book built issue equity shares IPODocuments`
  3. `Prospectus fixed price issue NSE Emerge SME "Limited" IPODocuments Prospectus dated 2026`
  4. `Red Herring Prospectus dated August 2026 SME lead manager registrar to the issue market maker`
  5. `Red Herring Prospectus dated June 2026 OR May 2026 OR April 2026 SME initial public offer`
  6. `Red Herring Prospectus 2025 NSE Emerge SME IPO "Please scan this QR Code to view the RHP"`
  7. `Red Herring Prospectus dated 2025 BSE SME Chapter IX ICDR Regulations 100% Book Building`
  8. `Prospectus dated 2026 SME IPO offer for sale fresh issue objects of the issue working capital Red Herring January February March 2026`
  9. `Red Herring Prospectus October 2026 SME IPO Emerge BSE SME bid opens October`
- **Why this host.** NSE and BSE terms forbid automated collection, so exchange-hosted copies are not used. Sharekhan serves the same documents as plain PDFs.
- **Bias.** The frame is what one search engine surfaced from one broker's library, over roughly 2024 to October 2026. It is not a random draw of all SME IPOs, and it may favour issues that brokers promoted.
- **Exclusions (decided from the cover text alone, before any check runs).** A document whose first five pages do not mention an SME or EMERGE platform is excluded as not an SME issue. A file identical (same SHA-256) to another in the list counts once. Excluded files are listed in the results, not hidden.

## Measures (phase A, no credits)
Run at code commit `2f1cd82` plus any fixes made during this test, which are listed in the results.

| Code | Measure | Counted as a success when |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Fetched | HTTP 200, PDF header, 40 MB or under (the intake limit) |
| A2 | Readable | at least 90% of pages carry text (no OCR needed) |
| A3 | Recognised | the document passes the prospectus check |
| A4 | Sections | all 7 target sections located (general information, objects, business, promoters, group companies, litigation, regulatory) |
| A5 | Issuer and date | the issuer name is not the "Unnamed issuer" fallback, and a prospectus date is read |

The headline is A4: "all seven sections located in X of N". Each partial result is reported as it is. The issuer name of every document is also checked by eye against its cover.

## Phase B: claim reading (model calls, no SerpApi credits)
For every prospectus that passes A4, the claim reader runs and counts what it grounds: vendor quotations, lead managers, places, disclosed cases, promoters. Five prospectuses, the first five by ascending SHA-256, get their objects section read by hand and compared with the extracted quotations, giving a small hand-checked accuracy figure. If the model quota runs out, the rest are listed as not run.

## Phase C: full scans (SerpApi credits)
- **Credits.** A new key with 250 searches. About 20 are held back, so the run stops when fewer than 24 remain.
- **Selection.** Prospectuses that pass A4 and were not scanned in an earlier audit, in ascending SHA-256 order. This fixes the order without any choice on my part.
- **Method.** The same as the six-prospectus survey: ruleset version 1, thresholds unchanged, evidence read as of each prospectus date, `--mode fixed` with `--max-credits 24`.
- **Flagged.** Any line that doesn't match. Each is then read by hand against its source and described as about the company, its vendor or its banker.
- **Not completed.** A scan that hits the cap before the card is built is reported as not completed, not re-run.
- **No ground truth.** None of these companies has a known problem. This measures how often Quaoar flags ordinary companies, not accuracy.

## Rules for what goes wrong
- A parsing failure found in phase A is fixed with a test first, with no rule or threshold changed. Phase A is then re-run on the same sample, and both results are reported.
- A bug found in phase C that does not affect the cards already produced is recorded and fixed afterwards.
- Nothing here is a claim that Quaoar detects fraud. The numbers describe reading coverage and how often ordinary companies are flagged.

## Amendment 1: a held-out sample, written after the first two runs and before the third
Phase A was run twice on the 34 files above. Both runs are reported in the results.

- **Run 1** (code `2f1cd82`): 23 of 34 documents had all seven sections located. Eleven did not: three printed their contents list as "TABLE OF CONTENT", "TABLE CONTENTS" or with underscore leaders; seven carried a title variant ("DEVLOPMENTS", "GROUP ENTITIES OF OUR COMPANY", "SECTION - V -", "DISCLOSURE"); one had a stray full stop in its contents list that skipped later sections. Two covers printed the issuer name in mixed case.
- **Fixes** (commits `fb14b7b`, `49a51c6`): each was written test-first. No rule or threshold changed.
- **Run 2** (code `9ba0557`): 33 of 34 have all seven located. The one that doesn't is NSE's own mainboard prospectus, which has no identifiable promoter and so no promoters section.
- **A screening error.** The script's SME screen only looked for the letters "SME" or "EMERGE" anywhere in the first five pages, which is looser than the rule above. Six of the 34 are mainboard issues listed on BSE and NSE, found by reading their listing statements: PR0068L, PR00675, PR0067J, PR0065D, PR0060O and PR0068E. Under the protocol's own rule they are excluded, which leaves 28 SME prospectuses. The script now looks for the listing statement naming the SME or EMERGE platform in the first twelve pages.
- **Why run 2 is not the headline.** The fixes were found on those same files, so run 2 measures how well the fixes fit them, not how the parser meets a prospectus it has not seen.

**The held-out run.** `coverage-2026-10-10-holdout.txt` lists 19 further final prospectuses, none among the 34, from ten more searches of `sharekhan.com`:
- `Red Herring Prospectus SME IPO textile OR garments OR apparel manufacturer Emerge OR "BSE SME" 100% book built`
- `... pharmaceuticals OR chemicals OR healthcare company SME platform fresh issue`
- `... engineering OR infrastructure OR construction OR steel company EMERGE Platform of NSE`
- `Prospectus SME IPO software OR technology OR digital OR services company SME platform of BSE book running lead manager`
- `Red Herring Prospectus 2024 SME IPO NSE Emerge OR BSE SME initial public issue dated 2024`
- `Prospectus 2025 SME IPO food OR agro OR packaging OR plastics OR electronics company BSE SME NSE Emerge market maker`
- `Red Herring Prospectus dated October 2025 OR November 2025 OR December 2025 ...`
- `Red Herring Prospectus dated March 2025 OR April 2025 OR May 2025 OR June 2025 ...`
- `Red Herring Prospectus dated 2023 SME IPO ...` and `Prospectus dated fixed price issue SME platform ...`
- `Red Herring Prospectus February 2026 OR March 2026 ...`, `... August 2025 ... logistics OR retail OR hospitality OR education OR media company`, `... hospital OR clinic OR diagnostics OR education OR consultancy ...`
- three name look-ups (Oval Projects Engineering, Manas Polymers and Energies, Tejas Cargo India), made to find a file that a snippet named but did not link

The parser is frozen at `9ba0557` for this run, and the same measures A1 to A5 apply, with the stricter SME screen. Nothing is fixed before the numbers are written down. Any failure it shows is reported as a failure, then fixed afterwards with a test, as before. The same-sample run (28 SME documents) and the held-out run are reported in separate rows.

## Amendment 2: the held-out run, and a second held-out run, written before it
- **Held-out run 1** (parser frozen at `9ba0557`, 19 files): all 19 fetched and passed the prospectus check. 18 are SME issues; one (PR005YE, Advance Agrolife) is a mainboard issue that the stricter screen excluded. **16 of 18 SME prospectuses had all seven sections located.** The two misses were a contents entry reading "OBJECTS FOR THE ISSUE" (the parser accepted only "OF") and a misspelt "OTHER REGULATORY AND STATUTORY DISCLSOURES". The issuer name was right in all 19 and a prospectus date was read in all 19.
- **Fix** (commit `f6119e3`): both variants, with tests.
- **Held-out run 2** (parser frozen at `f6119e3`): `coverage-2026-10-10-holdout2.txt` lists 6 further final prospectuses that none of the earlier runs contained. They came from five more searches (three named issuers whose drafts a snippet had shown, and three lists of lead managers). The same measures and the same SME screen apply. The searches are now returning almost only files already in the three lists, so the sample ends here.
- **What each number means.** The 28 same-sample documents measure the fixes' fit to the files they came from. The 18 and the 6 are files the parser had not been tuned on at the time they were run. The headline uses those two, and states the first run's misses.
