# Coverage test on real SME prospectuses, 10 Oct 2026

**Written before any file in this sample was downloaded or run.** The sample, the measures and the rules below were fixed first. Every result is reported, whatever it shows.

## Question
How many real SME prospectuses can Quaoar read end to end, and how often do its checks flag an ordinary company? The first part needs no SerpApi credit and no model call, so it can be run at scale.

## Sample
- **Frame.** Every final red herring prospectus or prospectus that nine fixed searches of `sharekhan.com` returned: the files coded `PR…`. Draft prospectuses (`PD…`) are left out. The listing is `coverage-2026-10-10-sample.txt`, in the order found: 34 PDFs under `/MediaGalary/IPODocuments/`.
- **Searches.** Run through a web search restricted to `sharekhan.com`:
  1. `IPO "RHP" pdf SME IPO 2026 Emerge prospectus`
  2. `Red Herring Prospectus BSE SME platform book built issue equity shares IPODocuments`
  3. `Prospectus fixed price issue NSE Emerge SME "Limited" IPODocuments Prospectus dated 2026`
  4. `Red Herring Prospectus dated August 2026 SME lead manager registrar to the issue market maker`
  5. `Red Herring Prospectus dated June 2026 OR May 2026 OR April 2026 SME initial public offer`
  6. `Red Herring Prospectus 2025 NSE Emerge SME IPO "Please scan this QR Code to view the RHP"`
  7. `Red Herring Prospectus dated 2025 BSE SME Chapter IX ICDR Regulations 100% Book Building`
  8. `Prospectus dated 2026 SME IPO offer for sale fresh issue objects of the issue working capital Red Herring January February March 2026`
  9. `Red Herring Prospectus October 2026 SME IPO Emerge BSE SME bid opens October`
- **Why this host.** NSE and BSE terms forbid automated collection, so exchange-hosted copies are not used. Sharekhan serves the same documents as plain PDFs.
- **Bias.** The frame is what one search engine surfaced from one broker's library, over roughly 2024 to October 2026. It is not a random draw of all SME IPOs, and it may favour issues that brokers promoted.
- **Exclusions (decided from the cover text alone, before any check runs).** A document whose first five pages do not mention an SME or EMERGE platform is excluded as not an SME issue. A file identical (same SHA-256) to another in the list counts once. Excluded files are listed in the results, not hidden.

## Measures (phase A, no credits)
Run at code commit `2f1cd82` plus any fixes made during this test, which are listed in the results.

| Code | Measure | Counted as a success when |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Fetched | HTTP 200, PDF header, 40 MB or under (the intake limit) |
| A2 | Readable | at least 90% of pages carry text (no OCR needed) |
| A3 | Recognised | the document passes the prospectus check |
| A4 | Sections | all 7 target sections located (general information, objects, business, promoters, group companies, litigation, regulatory) |
| A5 | Issuer and date | the issuer name is not the "Unnamed issuer" fallback, and a prospectus date is read |

The headline is A4: "all seven sections located in X of N". Each partial result is reported as it is. The issuer name of every document is also checked by eye against its cover.

## Phase B: claim reading (model calls, no SerpApi credits)
For every prospectus that passes A4, the claim reader runs and counts what it grounds: vendor quotations, lead managers, places, disclosed cases, promoters. Five prospectuses, the first five by ascending SHA-256, get their objects section read by hand and compared with the extracted quotations, giving a small hand-checked accuracy figure. If the model quota runs out, the rest are listed as not run.

## Phase C: full scans (SerpApi credits)
- **Credits.** A new key with 250 searches. About 20 are held back, so the run stops when fewer than 24 remain.
- **Selection.** Prospectuses that pass A4 and were not scanned in an earlier audit, in ascending SHA-256 order. This fixes the order without any choice on my part.
- **Method.** The same as the six-prospectus survey: ruleset version 1, thresholds unchanged, evidence read as of each prospectus date, `--mode fixed` with `--max-credits 24`.
- **Flagged.** Any line that doesn't match. Each is then read by hand against its source and described as about the company, its vendor or its banker.
- **Not completed.** A scan that hits the cap before the card is built is reported as not completed, not re-run.
- **No ground truth.** None of these companies has a known problem. This measures how often Quaoar flags ordinary companies, not accuracy.

## Rules for what goes wrong
- A parsing failure found in phase A is fixed with a test first, with no rule or threshold changed. Phase A is then re-run on the same sample, and both results are reported.
- A bug found in phase C that does not affect the cards already produced is recorded and fixed afterwards.
- Nothing here is a claim that Quaoar detects fraud. The numbers describe reading coverage and how often ordinary companies are flagged.

## Results

### Phase A: can it read real prospectuses? (no credits, no model)
59 files from `sharekhan.com`, 23,504 pages. Every file downloaded, passed the prospectus check, needed no OCR and had a cover date read. Eight turned out to be mainboard issues (listed on BSE and NSE) and are left out of the SME figures. That leaves **51 SME prospectuses**. The per-file table is in [`coverage-2026-10-10-files.md`](coverage-2026-10-10-files.md).

| Sample | SME files | All seven sections at first sight | After the fixes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Used to find the first fixes | 28 | 19 (68%) | 28 |
| Held-out 1, parser frozen at `9ba0557` | 18 | **16 (89%)** | 18 |
| Held-out 2, parser frozen at `f6119e3` | 5 | **5 (100%)** | 5 |
| **Files the parser had not been tuned on** | **23** | **21 (91%)** | 23 |
| All | 51 | 40 | **51** |

- **The number to quote is 21 of 23.** The "after the fixes" column measures how well the fixes fit the files they came from. It is not an independent result.
- **Issuer names.** Read as a plausible company name in 22 of the 23 unseen SME files. The miss named the registrar, because it was printed twice in capitals and the issuer once in mixed case on the first page. Fixed and re-checked on all 59.
- **What a miss looks like.** In every miss one section title was spelt or worded differently in the contents list ("TABLE OF CONTENT", underscore leaders, "DEVLOPMENTS", "GROUP ENTITIES OF OUR COMPANY", "OBJECTS FOR THE ISSUE", "DISCLSOURES"). No section was ever guessed. A missing section is reported as missing.
- **Mainboard issues.** All eight are read (seven of the eight have all seven sections; NSE's own prospectus has no promoter, so no promoters section). They are not counted above.

### Phases B and C: claims and full scans (model and search credits)
**This part is only partly valid, and the reason is a quota I did not check.** The two Cohere keys are trial keys, limited to 1,000 calls a month, and the calls made by earlier audits and tests this month count against it. About twelve files into the run the keys hit the limit. Every model call after that failed, so the claim reader returned nothing for 37 files, and the scans for those files ran with no claims read. Their cards show only the news line ("1 of 1 check out"), which reads like a complete check when nothing was read.

I stopped the run when I saw this. The records are classified by what the model actually read:

| Class | Files | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Read completely | 10 | claims grounded, at most 2 unread pieces |
| Read in part | 3 | some claims, many pieces unread |
| Not read | 37 | no claim read: model quota spent |
| Scan stopped at the cap | 1 | 24-credit cap reached before a card |

**What the valid evidence shows**

- **Claim reading, 10 files read completely:** lead managers found in 10, past issues in 9, disclosed cases in 8, promoters in 10, group companies in 6, places in 10, **vendor quotations in 7**. The grounding step dropped 227 of the model's proposals as not on the page or generic (101 of them vendor quotations), which is the guard working. I have not checked, for the three files with no quotation, whether their objects section names a vendor.
- **Full scans with a complete read: 6 cards**, 94 credits (15.7 each). 43 lines check out, 2 don't match, 26 couldn't be found.
- **Two flags among them, each read against its source:**
  - **Narmadesh Brass Industries, BK-04.** A real SEBI adjudication order of December 2023 naming its lead manager, Aryaman Financial Services Limited ([SEBI order](https://www.sebi.gov.in/enforcement/orders/dec-2023/adjudication-order-in-the-matter-of-aryaman-financial-services-limited_80286.html)). A real record, about the banker, not the company.
  - **Royal Arc Electrodes, VX-05: a false flag.** Google Maps matched "VRAJ Construction" at 964 Station Ave, Bensalem, Pennsylvania, to the Indian vendor "M/s Vraj Construction" and read it as permanently closed. It is the wrong company. Fixed in `686a236` with a test: a name match at a plainly foreign address is ignored.
- **One partial card also flags.** Oval Projects Engineering (only its lead manager was read): a real SEBI adjudication order of July 2023 naming SMC Capitals Limited ([SEBI order](https://www.sebi.gov.in/enforcement/orders/jul-2023/adjudication-order-in-the-matter-of-smc-capitals-limited_74853.html)). The two other partial cards (Accretion Pharmaceuticals, Everestims Technologies) have no flags.
- **Counted with the earlier audits,** ordinary companies with a completed card: 4 (survey) + 3 (controls) + 6 = **13**. Four had a non-matching line. Three are real public records (one court record, two SEBI orders about a lead manager) and one is a wrong-company match that is now fixed. No card flagged a company for fraud.

**What is not valid:** the 7 cards for files whose claims were not read (14 credits in all, 2 each), and the 37 claim-reading rows with no claims. They say nothing about those companies. They are not counted anywhere above, and the model's failure is the cause, not the prospectuses.

### Reading
- **Reading coverage is the result.** On SME prospectuses the parser had not been tuned on, all seven sections were located in 21 of 23. A miss is a differently worded contents entry, and no section is ever guessed.
- **The flag rate is a small, honest number.** Among 13 completed cards of ordinary companies, 3 had a real public record Quaoar surfaced and 1 had a wrong-company match that is now fixed. Nothing here measures accuracy, and there is no ground truth.
- **What is missing.** The credits would have covered roughly 15 to 20 cards. Six are valid. The rest need a model key with quota, have not been run, and are not claimed.
