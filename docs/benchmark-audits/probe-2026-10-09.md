# P0 probe, 2026-10-09

## Method
- 20 live SerpApi searches, all through `LedgerClient` (scan ids `probe-20261009*`), script `scripts/probe.py` plus two short follow-ups run the same way.
- Every response is in the local ledger; none is committed, because they hold third-party page content.
- Credit accounting check: the ledger counted 20 credits; SerpApi's account endpoint reported `this_month_usage: 20`, `plan_searches_left: 230`.

## Findings
| Question | Result | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Does Google honour `site:`? | No. Five Google queries with `site:` (single domain and OR of domains) came back mostly from other domains; a single `site:sebi.gov.in` query had 1 of 10 results on sebi.gov.in. `search_parameters` shows the query was sent unchanged. | Google is never trusted to restrict by site. |
| Which alternative works? | DuckDuckGo: 10 of 10 results on sebi.gov.in. Bing: 0 of 10 (an unrelated Q&A site). Google `as_sitesearch`: 1 of 10. Domain as a keyword: 2 of 9. | Every site-restricted search runs on `duckduckgo` and is post-filtered to the requested domains. Bing and `as_sitesearch` were removed from the query guard. |
| R1: do registry snippets carry the vendor fields? | DuckDuckGo with an OR of zaubacorp, instafinancials and tofler: 4 of 4 results on those sites. Snippets carry CIN, incorporation date, authorised capital, paid-up capital and status. No balance-sheet or AGM date. | VX-02, VX-03 and VX-05 are feasible. VX-04 (filing staleness) needs a follow-up registry query or stays UNVERIFIED. |
| R2: Maps coverage | Issuer offices: 3 of 5 targets matched exactly (similarity 1.0); the Trafiksol vendor matched 0.73 (partial); one issuer matched 0.56 (miss). Place fields include `open_state`, `type`, `rating`, `reviews` and, on some vendor candidates, `unclaimed_listing`. | Absence stays UNVERIFIED as designed. Closure is read only from `open_state` saying permanently or temporarily closed (daily hours say "Closed" on weekends). `unclaimed_listing` is recorded as an observation, never a status on its own. |
| R4: price data for SME stocks | Google's answer box returned exchange and price for an SME stock. `google_finance` was not tested, to save credits. | BK-06 stays P1; the answer box is the fallback. |
| R5: which evidence carries dates | News 39 of 39 dated; YouTube 13 of 13 dated (relative, resolved against the search time); Google organic 0 to 4 of 10 dated; registry snippets undated. | Strict point-in-time mode leans on news, video and order dates; registry and Maps evidence is marked undated. |

## Limits
- Small sample, one day; Google's behaviour can change.
- The `site:` result may belong to the same family as SerpApi issue #4396 (`site:` with `tbs`); it is worth reporting upstream with these request hashes.

## Follow-up: mistaken identity in the litigation check (2026-10-09)
The first live run of the litigation check on the Trafiksol prospectus flagged "5 matters" naming two promoters. All were namesakes (an Indian Kanoon case from 1908, an unrelated Rajasthan case). Name-only matching is unreliable for common Indian names, so:
- a promoter hit now counts only when the legal page also names the issuer;
- cards never print an individual's name, only "the issuer" or "a promoter";
- the re-run on the same prospectus reports no undisclosed matters, which matches what is known.
Regression tests: `tests/unit/checks/test_litigation.py`.
