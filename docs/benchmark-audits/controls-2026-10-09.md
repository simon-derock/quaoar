# Control prospectuses: does Quaoar stay quiet on ordinary companies? (2026-10-09)

## Rule, written before any control was scanned
- **Controls** are every other final prospectus linked on the same merchant banker's own website (Ekadrisht Capital, `ekadrisht.com/past-ipo-filings`) at the time of selection, apart from the Trafiksol positive: **Aelea Commodities, Indian Emulsifier, TBI Corn**. Peshwa Wheat, Speciality Medicines and Raviraj Process Controls were left out only because the site links no final prospectus for them.
- Nothing was dropped after seeing results. No SEBI order against any of the three was known to me at selection time; I did not check afterwards to decide inclusion.
- **Flagged** means at least one signal with status "doesn't match".
- Each prospectus is scanned once in agent mode with the cutoff set to the date printed on its cover, with the code and ruleset (`RULESET_VERSION = 1`) as committed before the scans.
- Positive: Trafiksol ITS Technologies (SEBI interim order 2024-10-11, refund order 2024-12-03, final order 2026-08).

## Known confound
All four issues share one banker, so banker lines (BK-04/05) are nearly identical across cases and cannot separate them. Only vendor, site, litigation and news lines can.
