    How it works
    
      
    From PDF to proof
    
      
    
        
    01
    Read
    Pull the checkable claims out of the prospectus.
    
        
    02
    Check
    Search for each one through SerpApi.
    
        
    03
    Prove
    Fixed rules set every status, with a source link.
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    What it looks at
    
      
    Five checks
    
      
    
        
    Vendor
    Is the company quoting for the money real, and in proportion?
    
        
    Premises
    Does the office or factory exist?
    
        
    Banker
    Any SEBI order naming the lead manager?
    
        
    Litigation
    Court matters the prospectus does not list.
    
        
    News
    Dated coverage, with promotion shown only as context.
