    From a PDF to proof, in a few minutes.
    
      
    
        
    01
    Read
    The prospectus is split into sections and its checkable claims are pulled out. A claim only counts if it is found on the page it cites.
    
        
    02
    Check
    Each claim is searched through SerpApi: company registry, Maps, SEBI orders, court records, news. Evidence is read as of the prospectus date.
    
        
    03
    Prove
    Fixed rules, not a language model, set every status. Each line links to its source with the search id, so anyone can repeat the check.
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    What it looks at.
    
      
    
        
    Vendor
    The company quoting for the IPO money: is it on the registry, active, is its paid-up capital in proportion to the quote, does any court or SEBI page name it.
    Registry pages · Google Maps
    
        
    Premises
    Does the registered office or factory exist on Maps, and is it open?
    Google Maps
    
        
    Merchant banker
    SEBI orders naming the lead manager or the issuers it brought before.
    SEBI orders
    
        
    Litigation
    Court or SEBI matters naming the issuer that the prospectus does not list.
    SEBI · Indian Kanoon · IBBI
    
        
    News and hype
    Dated coverage before the prospectus date. Promotion volume is shown as context, never as a finding.
    Google News · YouTube

      • Absence is not a finding. Small private companies barely appear online, so “couldn't find” is common and is never held against a company.

      • A screen, not a verdict. The 100× vendor-capital rule is a reason to look closer, not a conclusion.

      • A very small evaluation. One positive case in two versions and three controls. No accuracy claim is made.
