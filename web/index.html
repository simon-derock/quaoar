    Five checks on every prospectus

    Vendor
    Is the company quoting for the IPO money on the registry, active, and big enough for the quote?
    Premises
    Does the registered office or factory exist on Google Maps, and is it open?
    Lead manager
    Has SEBI issued an order naming the banker or the companies it brought to market?
    Litigation
    Are there court or SEBI matters that the prospectus doesn't list?
    News
    Was there adverse coverage before the prospectus date? Promotion volume is shown only as context.

    Questions

    What is Quaoar?

    A reality check for SME IPOs. It reads the company's prospectus, picks out the claims that can be checked, such as who quoted for the IPO money and where the factory is, and checks each one against public search results. Every line comes back as checks out, doesn't match or couldn't find, with its source.

    Who is it for?

    Anyone about to apply to a small-company IPO on NSE Emerge or BSE SME, and the analysts and journalists who read prospectuses for a living.

    Will it tell me whether to apply?

    No. It shows facts and where they came from. The decision stays with you.

    Where does the evidence come from?

    Every search goes through SerpApi: Google Maps, Google News, YouTube, and site-restricted searches of company registries, SEBI orders and court records. Each line keeps its SerpApi search id, so anyone can repeat it.

    What does a check cost?

    Watching a recorded case is free and needs no keys. A live scan of a new prospectus takes about 12 to 37 SerpApi searches.

    Can I trust a flag?

    A flag is a reason to look closer, not a conclusion. “Couldn't find” is never counted against a company. The evaluation is small: one known case in two versions and three ordinary companies, so no accuracy claim is made.

    Why the name?

    Quaoar is a small world beyond Neptune: real, but hard to see without the right instruments. Most SME issuers are like that too.
